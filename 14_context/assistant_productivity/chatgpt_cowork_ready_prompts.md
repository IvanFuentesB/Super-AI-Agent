# ChatGPT / Cowork Ready Prompts

## Morning dashboard

> Build a concise morning artifact from the latest Ghoti status, open PRs,
> handoffs, routines, and top-five actions. Separate "works now", "requires
> approval", and "future". Do not send messages or change accounts.

## Strategy-to-builder packet

> Turn this goal into one narrow Claude Code implementation packet and one
> separate Codex audit packet. Define branch, owned files, exclusions, checks,
> and done criteria. Avoid overlapping files.

## Connector readiness review

> Summarize operator-reported Gmail, Calendar, and Figma connector availability
> plus future GitHub, Notion, Ghoti-status, tool-stack, and PR-status views.
> Treat every connector as disabled in Ghoti runtime and propose read-only or
> draft-only uses first.

## Prompt and memory manager

> Convert the current conversation into reusable prompts, durable decisions,
> unresolved questions, and a compact next-session context packet. Exclude
> secrets, private identifiers, and stale duplicate instructions.

## Weekly opportunity review

> Compare the current content, business, and automation backlogs. Rank legal,
> ethical, low-cost supervised opportunities. No account creation, posting,
> spending, trading, mass messaging, or unsupported revenue claims.
